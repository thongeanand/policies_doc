<h2>1. Purpose and Commitment</h2>
<p>[App Name] is a live video and chat platform for adults. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion, trafficking of minors, and any content or behavior that sexualizes or endangers children. This policy explains the standards we publish, the tools we provide, and the actions we take to keep children safe.</p>

<h2>2. Scope</h2>
<p>This policy applies to every user, host, agency or talent manager, and employee or contractor connected to [App Name], and to all features of the app, including live video calls, live streams, chat messages, profile photos and videos, gifts, and any user-generated content.</p>

<h2>3. Our Standards Against CSAE</h2>
<p>The following are strictly prohibited on [App Name]:</p>
<ul>
<li>Any sexual or sexualized content depicting, or appearing to depict, a person under 18.</li>
<li>Creating, uploading, sharing, requesting, or linking to CSAM, including AI-generated or digitally altered imagery.</li>
<li>Grooming: building a relationship with a minor for sexual purposes or exploitation.</li>
<li>Sextortion: threatening or coercing a minor to produce or share sexual content.</li>
<li>Soliciting, advertising, or arranging the sexual abuse, trafficking, or exploitation of minors.</li>
<li>Sexual or suggestive comments directed at, or about, a minor.</li>
<li>Using the platform to contact, locate, or exploit a minor, including moving them to other platforms for this purpose.</li>
</ul>
<p>Accounts that violate these standards are permanently banned and reported to the relevant authorities.</p>

<h2>4. Age Requirement</h2>
<p>[App Name] is intended only for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly allow minors to use the app.</p>
<ul>
<li>Users must confirm they are 18 or older at sign-up.</li>
<li>Hosts must complete identity and age verification before they can go live or receive payouts.</li>
<li>If we learn that an account belongs to a person under 18, we immediately suspend it, remove related content, and review it for possible exploitation.</li>
<li>Parents or guardians who believe a minor is using the app can report it using the contact details in Section 9.</li>
</ul>

<h2>5. Prevention and Detection</h2>
<p>To prevent CSAE, we use a combination of:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Automated screening</strong> of profile photos, videos, and text for sexual content and signs of minors.</li>
<li><strong>Live stream and call moderation</strong> by trained human moderators, with the ability to interrupt or end sessions.</li>
<li><strong>Keyword and behavior detection</strong> to flag grooming, solicitation, and suspicious contact patterns.</li>
<li><strong>Host and agency vetting</strong>, including identity verification and agency accountability for the hosts they manage.</li>
<li><strong>Account enforcement</strong>, including blocking repeat offenders by device and payment identifiers where permitted by law.</li>
<li><strong>Moderator training</strong> on recognizing and escalating child safety concerns.</li>
</ul>

<h2>6. In-App Reporting and User Feedback</h2>
<p>Every user can report concerns directly inside the app:</p>
<ul>
<li>A <strong>Report</strong> option is available on user profiles, in chat, and during live calls and streams.</li>
<li>Users can select <strong>Child safety / minor involved</strong> as the reason and add details.</li>
<li>Reports are reviewed with the highest priority. Reports about possible child exploitation are escalated immediately to our child safety team.</li>
<li>Users can also <strong>block</strong> any other user at any time.</li>
<li>Users can contact us outside the app at vidya.thonge11@gmail.com (see Section 9).</li>
</ul>
<p>Reporters are not penalized for reports made in good faith.</p>

<h2>7. Handling CSAM and CSAE Reports</h2>
<p>When we detect or receive a report of suspected CSAE or CSAM, we will:</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Remove</strong> the content and disable access to it immediately.</li>
<li><strong>Suspend or permanently ban</strong> the accounts involved.</li>
<li><strong>Preserve</strong> relevant evidence (content, account data, logs) securely, as required by law, for use by law enforcement.</li>
<li><strong>Report</strong> the matter to the appropriate authorities, including:
<ul>
<li>Indian law enforcement and the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (<a href="https://cybercrime.gov.in">cybercrime.gov.in</a>);</li>
<li>The National Commission for Protection of Child Rights (NCPCR), where applicable; and</li>
<li>The National Center for Missing &amp; Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline or the relevant regional authority, as applicable.</li>
</ul></li>
<li><strong>Cooperate</strong> with law enforcement investigations and valid legal requests.</li>
</ol>
<p>We never copy, forward, or share CSAM except to report it to the authorities as required by law. Staff are instructed not to view CSAM beyond what is necessary for review and reporting.</p>

<h2>8. Legal Compliance</h2>
<p>[App Name] complies with applicable child safety and online safety laws in the countries where it operates, including, in India:</p>
<ul>
<li>The Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012, including its reporting obligations;</li>
<li>The Information Technology Act, 2000 (including Section 67B) and the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021; and</li>
<li>Other applicable laws and regulations on child protection and online content.</li>
</ul>
<p>We also comply with Google Play's Child Safety Standards and User Generated Content policies. We review this policy regularly and update it as laws and best practices change.</p>
